title: Great Models Think Alike and this Undermines AI Oversight
author: Shashwat Goel and eight co-authors (nine authors in the arXiv record)
---
Defines a similarity score for how much two models' mistakes overlap (CAPA) and reports that judges favour similar models, that mistakes get more similar as models get stronger, and that weak-to-strong training gains fall with similarity. Bears on whether different models' errors are independent and on judge or aggregator affinity. General multiple-choice evidence (MMLU-Pro, BBH).
