Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Models usually fail to turn a natural-language description into a TLA+ specification that the model checker accepts: the best pooled open-weight result was 26.6% parseable and 8.6% passing TLC, and GPT-5 passed TLC on 7 of 26.
