OSCA learns, on training problems, how to split a fixed sampling budget across several models, temperatures, prompts and languages, and is compared at equal compute with the best single configuration. Bears on claim 2 (different models against one model sampled more) with a general part (LiveBench: math, reasoning, language, data analysis) and a code part (LiveCodeBench, SWE-Bench Lite) that is recorded separately.
