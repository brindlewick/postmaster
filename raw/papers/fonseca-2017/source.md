author: Fonseca, Zhang, Wang, Krishnamurthy

Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Searching three machine-checked distributed systems for more than eight months found 16 bugs, none in the verified protocol logic, all in the specification, the unverified shim layer or the verification tools, and the authors trace them to assumptions the proofs relied on.
