Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A model driven by static analysis and Frama-C feedback wrote ACSL annotations (loop invariants, pre- and postconditions) that let Frama-C prove the benchmark's own assertion for 199 of 251 C programs.
