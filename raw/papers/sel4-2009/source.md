Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: The first full functional-correctness proof of a general-purpose OS kernel: 8,700 lines of C and 600 of assembler, about 200,000 lines of Isabelle proof script, about 20 person-years of proof effort (11 of them seL4-specific); the authors state in so many words that the proof shows the code matches the specification, not that the specification is what the user expects.
