title: Can Large Language Models Reason about Program Invariants?
author: Pei, Bieber, Shi, Sutton, Yin
---
Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Code models fine-tuned for invariant generation can predict program invariants statically, with the best result from a scratchpad approach that predicts invariants step by step through the program, at a quality the abstract calls comparable to a dynamic analysis tool given five traces.
