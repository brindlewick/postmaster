title: Talk Isn't Always Cheap: Understanding Failure Modes in Multi-Agent Debate
author: Andrea Wynn, Harsh Satija, Gillian Hadfield
---
Runs three-agent debates among GPT-4o-mini, LLaMA-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B in mixed and uniform groups and shows that debate can lower accuracy, including when the stronger model is in the majority. Bears on claim 2 (mixing a weaker model into a group of copies of the strongest) and claim 3 (reading peers' answers and revising). General tasks (CommonSenseQA, MMLU, GSM8K), 100 questions per task, 5 seeds. Read as HTML full text over two fetches; the arXiv comment says ICML MAS Workshop 2025.
