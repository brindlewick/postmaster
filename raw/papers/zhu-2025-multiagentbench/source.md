A benchmark and framework (MARBLE) for LLM multi-agent systems across six scenarios, one of them coding, comparing coordination protocols and planning strategies with milestone-based scores; it bears on group 6. In the text the fetch tool returned there is no single-agent baseline, so it cannot show whether several agents beat one.
