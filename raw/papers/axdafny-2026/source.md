Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: With a verifier-guided repair loop, models verified 56.4% of 250 competition-style Dafny problems (against 11.6% for one shot of GPT-5.5) and 92.7% of DafnyBench, but most verified solutions then failed the original executable tests, mainly by running out of time.
