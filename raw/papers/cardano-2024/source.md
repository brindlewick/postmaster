Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A team of about six formal-methods engineers, one in each development team of about six, applies proofs and executable Agda specifications to the pure inner layers of a Haskell blockchain node and tests the impure outer layers, checking the production code against a reference implementation generated from the specification.
