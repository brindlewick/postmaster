Preprint (cs.SE, v1) that has five vendors' coding-agent systems implement one specification many times, then measures failure overlap on one million random inputs and the effect of majority voting. It is the closest write-up found to "independent implementations by several vendors' agents" and bears directly on whether their errors are independent.
