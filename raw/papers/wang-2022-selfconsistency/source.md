The original self-consistency paper: sample many chain-of-thought paths from one model and take the majority answer. Bears on claim 2 (the same model sampled several times) and, through its Table 10, on claim 1 (a cross-model ensemble that did worse than one model). General arithmetic, commonsense and symbolic tasks only. Read through the ar5iv render over two fetches; peer reviewed (ICLR 2023 per the arXiv comment).
