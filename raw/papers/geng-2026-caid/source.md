A manager-plus-engineers scheme (CAID) in which a manager delegates dependency-aware subtasks to engineer agents, each in its own git worktree, and merges their branches with test-based checks, compared with one agent on Commit0-Lite and PaperBench with three models; it bears on group 6 and on whether isolation matters. Read as full text (HTML v2, two fetches) plus the abstract page; a preprint. The headline +25.6 and +14.7 points are the largest gains and both belong to one model.
