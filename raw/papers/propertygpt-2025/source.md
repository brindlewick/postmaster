Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A retrieval-augmented GPT-4 wrote Solidity invariants and pre/postconditions that matched human auditors' properties with 80% recall and 64% precision and, checked by a prover, found known and unknown vulnerabilities.
