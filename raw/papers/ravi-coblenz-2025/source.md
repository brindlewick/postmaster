title: An Empirical Evaluation of Property-Based Testing in Python
author: Ravi and Coblenz
---
Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A corpus study of 426 Python programs that use Hypothesis classifies their property-based tests into 12 categories and reports that, by mutation testing on 40 projects, each property-based test finds about 50 times as many mutations as the average unit test.
