Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: In Lean, with natural-language problems, the best model (o3) got code right 72.6% of the time, wrote a sound and complete specification 52.3% of the time, and proved the code against a specification only 4.9% of the time, one try each.
