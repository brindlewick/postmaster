title: Coding Agents Have Converged: Why the SWE-bench Leaderboard Can No Longer Order Its Top Entries, and What to Measure Instead

Audit of 254 public SWE-bench submissions that runs no model: it measures how much the sets of instances solved by top systems overlap. It bears on whether different systems' errors are independent and on how much a union or ensemble of leaderboard systems could add.
