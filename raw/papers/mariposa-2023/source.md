Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: In six existing verification projects, up to 5% of the solver queries behind their proofs are unstable, that is, a semantically irrelevant change such as renaming a variable can make verification slow or fail, and upgrading the solver often makes this worse.
