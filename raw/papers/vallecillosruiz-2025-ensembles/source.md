Preprint comparing ten small open models from five families, and three ensembles of them, on program repair and code generation, with five ways of picking 10 candidates out of 100. It bears on claim 1 (union of models against the best single model), claim 2 (a diverse set drawn across models against one model's own outputs) and claim 3 (which selection rule works).
