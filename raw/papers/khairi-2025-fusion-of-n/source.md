title: Making, not Taking, the Best of N
author: Ammar Khairi and three co-authors (four authors in the arXiv record)

Fusion-of-N (FusioN): a general LLM reads N candidate answers and writes one new answer, compared with Best-of-N selection (reward-model or LLM-judge), both for test-time scaling and for generating training data from a pool of five teacher models. Bears on claim 3 directly and on claim 2 through the single-teacher against five-teacher ablation. General tasks only (open-ended chat, translation, geography QA, grade-school math).
