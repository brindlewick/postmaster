Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: On 946 competitive-programming problems in Verus, the best of ten May 2026 models wrote correct code from prose 92.18% of the time, but a correct specification 48.31%, a proof 13.95% and a full verified program from prose 5.29%.
