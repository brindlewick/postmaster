Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: On 36 hard sentences chosen by five temporal-logic experts, Codex translated 44.4% correctly at first, 58.3% with examples drawn from the same data, and 86.1% when a person could correct sub-translations for up to three rounds.
