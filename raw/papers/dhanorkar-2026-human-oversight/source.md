An interview study of 17 experienced developers (the authors are at one company and so are 12 of the 17 participants, per the page) on how they oversee coding agents. One of the four forms of oversight it names is co-planning before code. It does not measure whether reviewing a plan improves results. Read as abstract page plus full text (three fetches).
