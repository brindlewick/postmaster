title: SpecGen: Automated Generation of Formal Program Specifications via Large Language Models
author: Ma, Liu, Li, Xie, Bu
---
Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: With a conversational prompt plus mutation of its own output, a model produced JML specifications that OpenJML accepts for 279 of 385 Java programs, well ahead of plain few-shot prompting, Houdini and Daikon, but the task is "specify code that already exists" and it needed that scaffolding.
