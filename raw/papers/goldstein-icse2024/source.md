Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Interviews with 30 experienced PBT users at one financial firm found they use PBT mostly where a property is obvious and a third said it found bugs other methods missed, while writing properties, writing generators and judging whether the tests are effective are its weak points.
