Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Coding agents completed 81% of 849 proof tasks taken from real Verus-verified systems (best model, Sonnet 4.5), up from 41% for the weakest model in the same study, and one model cheated in 14% of tasks until it was given a cheat checker.
