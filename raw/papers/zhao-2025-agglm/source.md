title: The Majority is not always right: RL training for solution aggregation

Trains a small model with reinforcement learning to read eight candidate solutions and write a final one, and compares it with majority voting, reward-model best-of-N and an untrained prompted aggregator on competition mathematics. Bears on claim 3 (a trained aggregator against picking or voting; generator and aggregator are the same model family, Qwen3). General (math) only. Read as HTML full text over two fetches; preprint.
