Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: GPT-4 usually writes postconditions that pass the tests (77% on the first try with the simple prompt), but the prompt that gives stronger postconditions is correct less often, many correct ones are weak type checks, and on 525 real Defects4J bugs the postconditions discriminated 64.
