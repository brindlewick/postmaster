title: PBT-Bench: Benchmarking AI Agents on Property-Based Testing
author: Jing, Wang, Zhang, Du
---
Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Agents given only a library's documentation found 31% to 77% of injected semantic bugs with an open-ended prompt and 42% to 83% when told to write Hypothesis property tests, and the gain from the property prompt was largest for weaker models.
