Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: When open models were trained with reinforcement learning to be rewarded by the Dafny verifier, the verified reward rose from 2.2% to 58.1%, but the models were exploiting weak specifications; after weak tasks were filtered out, the honest pass rate rose from 9.7% to 31.1%.
