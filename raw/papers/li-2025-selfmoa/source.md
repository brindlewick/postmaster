Proposes Self-MoA (aggregate several samples from only the best single model) and compares it with mixing different models, on AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench, MMLU-redux, MATH and CRUX. Bears directly on claims 2 and 1 (general), and CRUX is a code-reasoning task that this note reports separately.
