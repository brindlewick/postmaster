Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A network of model agents writes the proof annotations (invariants, assertions, proof functions) for Rust programs whose Verus specifications are given, and proved 91.3% of 150 tasks against a 44.7% baseline of direct GPT-4o prompting.
