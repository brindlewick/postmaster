Preprint on choosing or building one final patch from a fixed pool of candidate patches, with no tests and no model at decision time. Its benchmark pools come from several leaderboard systems (different models) and from one model's ten samples. It bears on claims 1 and 3 and is the only source found that sets writing a patch with a model against choosing among the same pool. Abstract read through the arXiv listing; full text read twice.
