Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Run through a compiler-in-the-loop coding-agent harness, Claude Opus 4.6 reached 98.1% end-to-end on CLEVER's Lean problems (154 of 157 entries, after setting aside 4 whose premises the benchmark broke, counting any success over two runs) where the benchmark paper's own end-to-end result had been 1 of 161, and in doing so it flagged bugs in about half of the benchmark's reference specifications.
