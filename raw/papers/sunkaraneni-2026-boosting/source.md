Preprint on sampling eight patches from one small model and choosing among them with critics and pairwise comparators, with a proof that selection cannot recover what no sample contains. Its only empirical benchmark is SWE-bench Verified. It bears on claim 3 (selection by the same model) and on shared blind spots. Abstract read through the arXiv listing; full text read twice; both reads said the text names no model for the critic and comparator roles, and the abstract says the same nano model is used.
