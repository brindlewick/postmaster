Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A survey of how large machine-checked proofs of programs are built, which says that scale has been reached only by small expert teams, that the cost literature finds proof effort tracking proof size, and that proof assistants and their extraction and linking steps are themselves part of what must be trusted.
