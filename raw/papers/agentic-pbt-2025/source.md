Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A coding agent that reads a Python module, infers properties from code, docstrings and usage, writes Hypothesis tests and triages the failures produced 984 bug reports over 100 packages, and of 50 reports two authors scored, 56% were valid and 32% worth reporting.
