Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A wrapper that stops the model from touching the program's logic, prunes needless invariants and adds fixed proof strategies lifted Claude 3.5 Sonnet from about 70% to 86% on DafnyBench, and tuned 7B and 14B open models reached 68% and 70%.
