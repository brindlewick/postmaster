A preprint case study at a large bank in which one staff engineer, with four AI agents (different products, in sequence) under a spec-first workflow, delivered a four-person initiative in about half the planned time. The engineer reviewed each generation against the specification produced in the step before. It is the source of the "50% time-to-market" figure that another preprint repeats. Software-engineering evidence (one case, historical baseline, no control group). Read as abstract page plus full text (three fetches).
