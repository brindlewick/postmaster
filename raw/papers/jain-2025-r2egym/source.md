Preprint on training a 32B open-weights agent and choosing among 26 of its candidate patches with two kinds of verifier, one that runs tests an agent wrote and one trained to score trajectories. It bears on claim 3 (verification against picking at random; combining two different verifiers beats either), with verifiers from the same model family as the agent.
