Preprint on a module that takes the draft patches of an existing repair system, samples further candidate patches, has a second model review them and has a model merge or choose among the partial fixes into one patch. It bears on claim 3 (a model reads several candidates and writes one) but does not compare against choosing one from the same pool. Abstract read through the arXiv listing; full text read twice.
