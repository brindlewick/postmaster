Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A full functional-correctness proof of a roughly 10,000-line C microkernel, extended to security properties and the compiled binary, took about 20 person-years of proof effort against 2.2 for building the kernel, found 144 defects in the C code, and rests on stated assumptions (assembly, boot code, caches, hardware) that it does not prove.
