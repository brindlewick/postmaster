title: Correlated Errors in Large Language Models
author: Elliot Kim and three co-authors (four authors in the arXiv record)

Measures how often two LLMs give the same wrong answer when both are wrong, over hundreds of models on two leaderboards and a resume-screening task, and what that does to LLM-as-judge scores. Bears on whether different models' errors are independent (general multiple-choice evidence, no code). Read as HTML full text over two fetches; peer reviewed (ICML 2025 per the arXiv comment).
