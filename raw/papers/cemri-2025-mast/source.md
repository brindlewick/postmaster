A failure taxonomy (MAST) and a set of annotated traces (MAST-Data) from seven multi-agent frameworks, including ChatDev and MetaGPT on programming tasks; it bears on group 6 as a measured account of how multi-agent flows fail, and on the case for explicit verification.
