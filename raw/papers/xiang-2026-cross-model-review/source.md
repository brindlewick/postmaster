A small controlled experiment with two vendors' coding agents on 116 competitive-programming tasks: each alone, each reviewing the other's draft, and each reviewing its own. It is the closest controlled measurement found for claim 4 and for claim 2 in a review role, in code. Accepted at a workshop (Agentic SE at KDD 2026, per the abstract page); v1 only. Abstract page read once; full text read twice with different prompts.
