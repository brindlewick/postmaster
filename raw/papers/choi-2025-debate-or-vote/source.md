title: Debate or Vote: Which Yields Better Decisions in Multi-Agent Large Language Models?
author: Hyeong Kyu Choi and two co-authors (three authors in the arXiv record)
---
Separates multi-agent debate into majority voting and inter-agent debate on seven benchmarks with small open models and argues, with a martingale result, that voting accounts for most of the gain. Bears on claim 3 (reading others' answers against voting) and on how much debate adds. General tasks. Its "heterogeneous agents" are different personas of one model, not different models. Read as HTML full text over two fetches; peer reviewed (NeurIPS 2025 spotlight per the arXiv comment).
