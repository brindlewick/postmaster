Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: On 427 Lean code-verification tasks with program and specification given, a specialised 8B proving model succeeded on 62.0%, against 23.8% for the best other model in the comparison, and general frontier models scored below about 24% on the same tasks; the baselines ran at pass@128 and the new system used up to 128 decomposition iterations, so the budgets differ.
