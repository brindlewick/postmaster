Preprint that takes one answer from each of 14 different models on HumanEval and LiveCodeBench and picks the program most similar to the others, by syntax and by behavioural equivalence. It bears on claim 1 (a pool of different models against the best model, choosing by consensus) and on claim 3 (selection only; the method cannot write a new program).
