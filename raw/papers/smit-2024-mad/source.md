Benchmarks several multi-agent debate strategies against self-consistency, Medprompt-style ensembling and a single agent on medical and reasoning QA, mostly with one model (gpt-3.5-turbo). Bears on claim 3 in the debate form (reading others' answers and revising) and on whether debate beats voting. General QA only.
