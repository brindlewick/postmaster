Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: In six Isabelle proof developments the length of a proof grows roughly with the square of the size of the property it proves, and statements that say more than they need inflate that size, so a statement's size is a leading, though not yet predictive, indicator of proof effort.
