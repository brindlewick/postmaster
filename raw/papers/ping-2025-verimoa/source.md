Preprint on hardware-description-language (Verilog) generation with layers of proposer agents and an aggregator, all the same LLM within a run, using two intermediate-language paths (C++ and Python) and a cache of the best candidates by simulation score. A lead in the package brief; it bears on claim 3 (an aggregator writes from ranked candidates) and does not test mixing different LLMs. Abstract read through the arXiv listing; full text read three times, with different prompts.
