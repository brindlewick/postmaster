Preprint (PerfOrch) that sends each generation, debugging and refinement step to the model ranked best for that language and problem category by offline profiling, trying up to five models in turn until the evaluation tests pass. It bears on claim 1 (different models per stage against the best single-model pipeline), with an acceptance rule that uses the full test suite.
