Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: The model proposes input-output tests, a person answers yes or no to each, and the answers prune and rank code candidates, lifting Codex's pass@1 by 22-38 points on MBPP and 25-54 points on HumanEval within one to five questions, which in the paper's evaluation were answered by simulated users.
