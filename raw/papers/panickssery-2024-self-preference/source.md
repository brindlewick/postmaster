title: LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations
author: Arjun Panickssery and two co-authors (three authors in the arXiv record)
---
Tests whether an LLM judge can recognise its own summaries and whether that goes with scoring them higher, with GPT-4, GPT-3.5 and Llama 2 on XSUM and CNN/DailyMail. Bears on whether an aggregator or judge from the same model family as the generators can be trusted (general summarization evidence only). Read as HTML full text over two fetches; preprint, no venue in the arXiv record.
