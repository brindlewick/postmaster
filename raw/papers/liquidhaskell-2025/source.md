Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Interviewing and observing 19 developers using LiquidHaskell found nine usability barriers; among them developers struggled to say what to prove, wrote post-conditions weaker than they should be, and could not tell from a passing check whether the specification was too weak.
