Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A pipeline that has a model write Dafny specifications for Python solutions and then screens them with test-based lemmas produced 2.7K verified Dafny programs; fine-tuning a 7B model on them lifted DafnyBench from about 32% to about 57%.
