Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Refined TypeScript is a lightweight refinement type system for TypeScript that enables static verification of higher-order, imperative programs, evaluated on parts of the Octane benchmarks, D3, Transducers and the TypeScript compiler.
