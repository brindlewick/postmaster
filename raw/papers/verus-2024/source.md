Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: Verus checks proofs about Rust programs with an SMT solver and the authors report 6.1K lines of verified Rust against 31K lines of proof across five systems, a proof-to-code ratio of 5.1 overall and up to 13.3 for the page table.
