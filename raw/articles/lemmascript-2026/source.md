Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A tech-preview toolchain verifies the pure, functional core of ordinary TypeScript carrying `//@ ` specification annotations by generating Dafny or Lean 4 code from it, and its own case studies verify parts of real packages in place.
