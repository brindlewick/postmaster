Captured for #300's research on functional programming, formal verification and AI coding: A formal-methods researcher used Claude Code to formalise a published concurrency logic in Lean 4, found that it can write proofs but sometimes thrashes or makes deep conceptual mistakes that do not cause a build error, and judged it slower than doing it by hand.
