6. How claude saves its config.
   Read from the claude 2.1.283 executable, which bundles proper-lockfile. Its config save takes
   proper-lockfile's lock on the config file with lockfilePath "<file>.lock", a directory, on the
   library's defaults: stale once its mtime is 10 s old, refreshed every 5 s by its holder. While
   the lock is held elsewhere it retries with a backoff; a stale one is removed and taken. Under the
   lock it re-reads the file, applies its change to what it read, writes the file with mode 0600,
   and releases the lock.
   scripts/launch.sh --self-test runs the race this closes, against a scratch config: a session
   that takes the lock, reads, holds 1.5 s and writes what it read. The trust step, taking the same
   lock, waited, and both the session's change and the trust entry landed. The trust step as first
   committed, with no lock, lost its trust entry to that session's write. With the session skipping
   the lock instead, the trust entry was lost the same way.
